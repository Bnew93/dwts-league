Season 35

The thirty-fourth season of Dancing with the Stars will premiere on ABC and Disney+ on September 16, 2025.[1] This season, marking the twentieth anniversary of the series, will be the third to air live on both networks simultaneously, and was the first of ABC's unscripted programs to earn an official renewal for their 2025–26 schedule.[2] Alfonso Ribeiro will return to host the season, while former professional dancer and judge Julianne Hough will return as co-host.[3]

Cast

[edit]

Couples

[edit]

On April 22, 2025, during an upfront for Hulu's unscripted programming, Robert Irwin was announced as the first celebrity participant for the thirty-fourth season.[4] He is the younger brother of season twenty-one winner, Bindi Irwin.[5] Alix Earle joined the cast one month later.[6] On June 30, during The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives' second season reunion special,[7] host Nick Viall revealed that Jen Affleck and Whitney Leavitt were joining Dancing with the Stars.[8] Affleck announced the birth of her third child two days later;[9] she was eleven weeks postpartum when the season premiered.[10] On August 25, Entertainment Tonight reported that Hilaria Baldwin joined the cast; People confirmed her appearance the following day.[11] The full roster of celebrity participants and their partnerships was officially revealed on Good Morning America on September 3.[12][13]

According to Parade, Artem Chigvintsev was not slated to return as a professional dancer this season.[14] Entertainment journalist Kristyn Burtt announced that Mark Ballas,[15] Britt Stewart, Brandon Armstrong, Rylee Arnold, Ezra Sosa, Emma Slater, Valentin Chmerkovskiy, Daniella Karagach, Pasha Pashkov, Alan Bersten, Witney Carson, Gleb Savchenko,[16] and the reigning champion Jenna Johnson were slated to return.[17] Ballas will compete for the first time since winning the thirty-first season with Charli D'Amelio.[15] Before his appearance was confirmed, Savchenko implied that he was not invited back to Dancing with the Stars amid cheating allegations from his ex-girlfriend and season thirty-three partner, Brooks Nader.[18] Jan Ravnik, former backup dancer for Taylor Swift's Eras Tour, joined the series as a first-time pro.[19] Although Sasha Farber did not return as a pro this season, he was involved with its social media marketing campaign.[20]

Hosts and judges

[edit]

Alfonso Ribeiro and Julianne Hough returned as hosts for the season.[21] Derek Hough, Carrie Ann Inaba and Bruno Tonioli returned as judges.[22] Inaba missed the season premiere due to illness; there was no replacement judge in her place.[23]

Dance troupe

[edit]

Hailey Bills, Onye Stevenson, Carter Williams, and Jaxon Willard joined the show as first-time troupe members.[24] Bills is the niece of Jenna Johnson and a former pro on Dancing with the Stars: Juniors.[24] Williams was a top six finalist on the seventeenth season of So You Think You Can Dance.[24] Willard was a contestant on the second season of World of Dance.[24]

Individual judges' scores in the charts below (given in parentheses) are listed in this order from left to right: Derek Hough, Bruno Tonioli.
